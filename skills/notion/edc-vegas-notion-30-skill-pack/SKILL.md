# EDC Vegas Notion 30 Skill Pack

## Purpose
A clone-ready pack of 30 compact skills for building and improving a Notion travel, festival, creator, and research workspace.

## How to use
Pick the numbered module that matches the task, follow its workflow, then end with Updates Made and Next Best Moves.

## 01 Notion Database Architect
Use for building a new database system. Identify entities, split by lifecycle, define properties, add starter rows, and add views.

## 02 Notion Dashboard Designer
Use for building a clean home page. Add quick links, today view, upcoming view, budget view, open questions, and update log.

## 03 Notion View Builder
Use for table, board, calendar, gallery, list, and timeline views. Filter by status, owner, date, category, and priority.

## 04 Notion QA Auditor
Use for workspace cleanup. Check missing fields, duplicate pages, unclear statuses, bad views, placeholders, and broken links.

## 05 Notion Import Mapper
Use for moving spreadsheets or markdown into Notion. Map columns to properties, clean statuses, and create import-ready tables.

## 06 Trip Itinerary Builder
Use for multi-day trips. Create one row per day with morning, afternoon, evening, late night, transportation, outfit, and notes.

## 07 Trip Budget Planner
Use for travel budgets. Track estimated cost, actual cost, who paid, category, date, receipt link, and refund status.

## 08 Outfit Planner
Use for trip outfits. Track person, use case, day, shoes, accessories, bag, comfort risk, purchase link, cost, and packed status.

## 09 Packing System Builder
Use for packing checklists. Group by documents, clothing, tech, toiletries, medicine, snacks, event gear, and emergency items.

## 10 Restaurant Research Tracker
Use for food lists. Track cuisine, meal, area, price, reservation status, must-order item, link, and saved social source.

## 11 Nightlife Planner
Use for clubs, lounges, shows, and casino nights. Track venue, date, cost, dress code, ticket link, and transportation notes.

## 12 Pool Party Planner
Use for day clubs and pool days. Track venue, headliner, dress code, cover, guest list, table option, bag rules, and timing.

## 13 Casino Planner
Use for casino visits. Track casino, rewards card, games, table minimum notes, food nearby, photo spots, and budget.

## 14 EDC Logistics Planner
Use for EDC prep. Track tickets, wristbands, shuttle, bag policy, meeting spot, water plan, set conflicts, and exit plan.

## 15 Artist Priority Planner
Use for festival lineups. Rank artists as must-see, strong option, maybe, or skip. Add stage, set time, conflicts, and notes.

## 16 Festival Safety Checklist
Use for comfort and safety. Track hydration, shoes, earplugs, ID, medication, chargers, layers, meeting points, and recovery plan.

## 17 Creator Content Planner
Use for content trips. Plan reels, TikToks, photos, captions, shot lists, hooks, locations, outfit changes, and posting dates.

## 18 Brand Outreach CRM
Use for partner outreach. Track brand, contact, platform, email, status, date sent, follow-up date, owner, and deliverables.

## 19 Email and DM Follow-Up
Use for outreach follow-up. Create polite follow-ups, log sent dates, mark next action, and avoid duplicate messages.

## 20 Reservation Timeline
Use for booking deadlines. Track hotel, flights, restaurants, clubs, shows, pool parties, tickets, transportation, and reminders.

## 21 Photo Spot Scout
Use for visual planning. Track location, best time, outfit notes, cost, indoor/outdoor, lighting, and content concept.

## 22 Couple Trip Planner
Use for two-person trips. Track shared must-dos, each person's priorities, date nights, matching outfits, photos, and memories.

## 23 Spotify Playlist Curator
Use for trip music. Build playlist briefs for travel, pool, club, casino, recovery, pregame, and festival energy.

## 24 Research Source Auditor
Use after research. Separate confirmed facts, likely options, rumors, outdated info, and items needing official confirmation.

## 25 Deep Research Synthesizer
Use when long research returns. Extract facts, sources, recommendations, risks, booking windows, and Notion update actions.

## 26 GitHub Skill Pack Builder
Use for reusable skills. Create folder, SKILL.md, purpose, triggers, workflow, QA checklist, output format, and handoff notes.

## 27 Claude Handoff Writer
Use for multi-agent continuation. Summarize current state, source of truth, files changed, next steps, and what not to touch.

## 28 Public-Safe Redaction
Use before committing docs. Remove personal emails, confirmation numbers, payment details, account numbers, and private URLs.

## 29 Notion Update Log Maintainer
Use after every edit. Record date, changes, source, open questions, and next suggested update.

## 30 Final Trip OS Polish
Use before sharing. Improve names, remove clutter, check mobile readability, add missing rows, clarify placeholders, and add next actions.

## Output format
### Updates Made
- Notion changes
- GitHub changes
- Research added
- Items still needed

### Next Best Moves
- List the next 3 to 7 actions.
