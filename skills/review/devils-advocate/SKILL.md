# Devil's Advocate

## When to use
Invoke this skill when you want me to:
- Challenge your assumptions
- Find weaknesses in a plan before you commit
- Argue the strongest opposing case
- Identify blind spots and risks

## How it works
I will take a contrarian but constructive stance, arguing against your position to help you stress-test ideas. I will not simply agree — I will find the strongest counterarguments.

## Trigger phrases
- "play devil's advocate"
- "challenge this"
- "what's wrong with my plan"
- "steelman the opposite"
